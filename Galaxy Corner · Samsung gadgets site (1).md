# The gadgets I keep coming back to

Specs change every year, so I write about how things feel to live with. Always check Samsung’s official site for the latest details before you buy.

📱

### Galaxy S series

The all-rounder. If a friend asks for one phone that does everything well, this is where I point them. The camera is the reason most people stay.

Good for: people who want one great phone and no fuss.

📖

### Galaxy Z Fold

A phone that opens into a small tablet. It felt like a gimmick to me until I read a long article on it in bed. Then I got it.

Good for: readers, multitaskers, show-offs (in a nice way).

📲

### Galaxy Z Flip

Small enough to vanish in a pocket, and the little cover screen saves you from picking up your phone every five minutes.

Good for: pocket-size lovers and selfie fans.

⌚

### Galaxy Watch

I started wearing it for notifications and stayed for the sleep and workout tracking. It’s nice to glance at your wrist instead of falling into your phone.

Good for: staying on top of your day without staring at a screen.

💍

### Galaxy Ring

A tracker you forget you’re wearing. Quiet, simple, and surprisingly comfortable to sleep in.

Good for: anyone who finds watches bulky.

🎧

### Galaxy Buds

Great for commutes, calls and those moments when the whole world is too loud. Pairing with a Galaxy phone takes about a second.

Good for: daily commuters and call-takers.

📝

### Galaxy Tab

The one I grab for sketching, watching shows, and taking notes with the S Pen. It’s the couch gadget that sneaks into work too.

Good for: students, note-takers and doodlers.

🏠

### SmartThings

The app that ties your lights, TV and appliances together. Set it up once on a lazy Sunday and your home starts feeling a little bit magic.

Good for: curious tinkerers who like their home to help out.
